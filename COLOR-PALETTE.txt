SWAPR COLOR PALETTE
Approved palette: teal + mint + warm white, with coral used sparingly.

Deep teal     #0F766E  Primary buttons, active navigation, selected filters
Soft mint     #DDF4EE  Secondary buttons, selected cards, icon backgrounds
Warm white    #FAFAF7  Screen background
White         #FFFFFF  Cards, inputs, bottom navigation
Charcoal      #202A29  Headings and primary text
Slate grey    #64716E  Supporting text and inactive navigation
Light grey    #E3E8E5  Borders and dividers
Warm coral    #F4846B  Celebration accents; avoid white text on coral

Keep the main navigation teal even when Leon changes category colors.
Use white text on deep teal buttons. Use charcoal/teal text on mint.
Source of truth in code: swapr-app/src/constants/palette.ts
Sample photo sources: swapr-app/assets/items/SOURCES.txt
